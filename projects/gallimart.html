GalliMart

An Android hyperlocal marketplace connecting nearby buyers, shopkeepers, and delivery partners through real-time inventory, ordering, and tracking.

StatusCompleted
PlatformAndroid
Year2025
RoleFull-Stack
Think of GalliMart as your neighborhood market, digitized—built for buyers, shopkeepers, and delivery partners within a single radius.

Why I Built It

Local shops rarely have a digital storefront, and buyers default to large e-commerce apps even for everyday nearby purchases. I wanted a lightweight marketplace that keeps commerce hyperlocal—connecting the three people already involved in any local sale: the buyer, the shopkeeper, and whoever delivers it.

What Makes It Different

GalliMart is role-aware from the ground up. Shopkeepers manage live inventory, buyers browse and order from nearby stores, and delivery partners see routes and order history—all synced instantly through Firebase so every role sees the same order state in real time.

Challenges

  • Designing three distinct role-based experiences in one app
  • Structuring Firebase Realtime Database for live inventory and orders
  • Keeping order status synced across buyer, shopkeeper, and driver
  • Building an intuitive add/edit flow for shopkeeper inventory
  • Handling authentication cleanly across three user types
  • Surfacing driver location and delivery history without clutter

Lessons Learned

  • Role-based design forces clarity earlier in the build
  • Realtime data models need discipline, not just speed
  • Marketplaces are as much about trust as they are about UI
  • Small, well-scoped screens beat one crowded dashboard
  • Local commerce has different constraints than general e-commerce

Key Features

What GalliMart Does

The core capabilities that keep hyperlocal commerce fast, current, and trustworthy.

Product Discovery

Buyers browse nearby stores and search live inventory across shopkeepers, so what they see reflects what's actually in stock right now.

Inventory Management

Shopkeepers add, edit, and track stock directly from their phone. Every change syncs instantly to Firebase and appears in buyer searches immediately.

Order Management & Tracking

Orders move through a shared real-time state—placed, confirmed, out for delivery, completed—visible consistently to buyer, shopkeeper, and driver.

Multi-Role Experience

Separate, purpose-built flows for buyers, shopkeepers, and delivery partners, backed by Firebase Authentication so each role only sees what it needs.
